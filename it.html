Our Story

Technology That Makes a Difference

Citiline Technologies Private Limited was founded with a singular vision: to make enterprise-grade technology accessible to businesses of all sizes. Headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, we have grown from a small IT consulting firm into a full-spectrum technology services company.

Over eight years, we have partnered with startups, SMEs, and large enterprises across fintech, retail, healthcare, manufacturing, and education — delivering solutions that don't just solve today's problems but prepare businesses for tomorrow.

Our team of 50+ certified professionals brings together deep expertise in cloud computing, software engineering, cybersecurity, AI, and strategic consulting — all under one roof.
